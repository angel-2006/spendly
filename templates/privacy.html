Privacy Policy

Here is how Spendly handles your information.

1. Data We Collect

When you register, we collect your name and email address. As you use Spendly, we store the expense details you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to operate Spendly: to sign you in, to record your expenses and to show you summaries of your spending. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and is accessible only to your account. Passwords are stored in hashed form, never as plain text. You can request deletion of your account and data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when a page loads. We do not share your expense data with any third party.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or about your data, please get in touch with the Spendly team through the contact details provided on our website.
